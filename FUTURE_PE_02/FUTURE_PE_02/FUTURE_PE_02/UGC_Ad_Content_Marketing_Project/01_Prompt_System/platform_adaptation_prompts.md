# Platform Adaptation Prompts

## Instagram Reels
Rewrite the UGC ad for a 20–30 second Instagram Reel.
Use a fast first-second visual, conversational language, short sentences and 3–5 on-screen text moments.
End with a simple CTA.

## Facebook / Meta Ads
Rewrite the same concept for a Meta paid ad.
Keep it creator-style but make the product and benefit clear.
Use a strong opening, problem-solution structure and a direct CTA.
Avoid exaggerated claims.

## YouTube Shorts
Rewrite the same concept for a 25–35 second YouTube Short.
Use a curiosity-driven opening, slightly clearer explanation and a memorable final CTA.

## Brand Voice Adapter
Adapt the script to:
1. Friendly Gen-Z
2. Minimal and premium
3. Expert-but-accessible
4. Casual Indian creator
5. Local-business conversational

Preserve the verified product facts and do not create unsupported claims.
