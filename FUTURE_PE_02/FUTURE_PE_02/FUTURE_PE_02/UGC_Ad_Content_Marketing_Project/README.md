# AI Content Marketing Using UGC Ads

## Project Title
**AI-Powered UGC Ad Content System for The Derma Co 2% Sali-Cinamide Anti-Acne Face Wash**

## Objective
Build a reusable prompt-engineering system that generates short-form, creator-style paid-ad concepts for Instagram Reels, Facebook/Meta Ads and YouTube Shorts.

## Real Product Chosen
**The Derma Co 2% Sali-Cinamide Anti-Acne Face Wash with 2% Salicylic Acid & 2% Niacinamide – 80ml**

The official product page currently describes it as helping clear acne and marks and remove excess oil. It lists 2% salicylic acid and 2% niacinamide as key ingredients and gives a listed 80ml price of ₹229 at the time of research. Prices/promotions can change.

Official source:
https://thedermaco.com/products/sali-cinamide-anti-acne-face-wash-with-2-salicylic-acid-2-niacinamide-80ml

## Target Audience
- Young adults interested in simple skincare routines
- People concerned about excess oil and acne-prone skin
- Short-form video audiences
- Indian D2C/skincare shoppers

## Core Problem
The ad needs to communicate a skincare problem quickly without sounding like a traditional polished commercial.

## Core Framework
**Hook → Problem → Product Introduction → Benefit/Feature → Soft Proof/Reason → CTA**

## Key Deliverables
- Reusable master prompt
- Brand/platform adaptation prompts
- 20 hooks
- 5 UGC-style ad scripts
- CTAs
- Captions
- Content matrix
- Instagram/LinkedIn sharing copy
- Documentation and prompt logic

## Important UGC Rule
These are **creator-style mock ad scripts**, not genuine customer testimonials. Do not publish statements such as “I used this and my acne disappeared” unless a real creator can truthfully substantiate the experience.

## Repository Structure
- `01_Prompt_System/` – reusable prompts
- `02_Product_Research/` – product facts and source
- `03_Hooks/` – hook library
- `04_Ad_Scripts/` – finished scripts
- `05_CTA_Captions/` – CTA and caption bank
- `06_Social_Sharing/` – mandatory social-post drafts
- `07_GitHub_Documentation/` – project methodology and checklist

## How to Reuse
Replace the product fields in `01_Prompt_System/master_prompt.md` with another product or business. Keep the same output structure.
