# Product Brief

## Product
The Derma Co 2% Sali-Cinamide Anti-Acne Face Wash with 2% Salicylic Acid & 2% Niacinamide – 80ml

## Verified product-page points used in this project
- Product positioning: anti-acne face wash.
- Brand page states it helps clear acne and marks.
- Brand page states it removes excess oil.
- Key ingredients listed: 2% Salicylic Acid and 2% Niacinamide.
- Brand page explains salicylic acid as helping dissolve debris clogging pores and helping reduce acne, blackheads and whiteheads.
- Brand page describes niacinamide as helping fade dark spots and even skin tone.
- Listed 80ml price at research time: ₹229, with MRP ₹249. Pricing may change.

## Ad-Safety Notes
Do not turn product-page descriptions into medical guarantees.
Do not promise that the product will cure acne.
Do not invent before/after results.
Do not write a fake first-person testimonial.
Current price/offer must be checked immediately before publication.
