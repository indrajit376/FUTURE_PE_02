# LinkedIn Sharing Post

I completed an AI-powered UGC ad content project focused on short-form paid social content.

**Business/Product:** The Derma Co 2% Sali-Cinamide Anti-Acne Face Wash

**Problem:** Traditional product copy can sound too promotional. The project focuses on creator-style scripts that communicate the product naturally while keeping the conversion goal clear.

**What I built:**
- Reusable UGC prompt system
- 20 hooks
- 5 ad scripts
- CTA and caption bank
- Platform adaptation prompts
- Product research/claim checklist
- GitHub-ready documentation

The biggest learning was that good AI marketing prompts need both creative direction and factual constraints.
